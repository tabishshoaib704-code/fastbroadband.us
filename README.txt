FastBroadbands.us - static site
Files: index.html, privacy.html, terms.html, disclaimer.html, assets/style.css, assets/app.js

Before launch:
1. assets/app.js - put your EmailJS publicKey, serviceId, templateId at the top.
   Template variables available: name, phone, email, zip, service, page, submitted_at.
2. Replace [YOUR EMAIL] and [YOUR BUSINESS ADDRESS] in privacy.html and terms.html.
3. Upload the folder to any static host (Netlify, Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, cPanel).
